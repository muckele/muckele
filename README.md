## About Me

I'm a Full-Stack Software Engineer and Product Builder who creates reliable applications at the intersection of software, AI, automation, and real business workflows. I work across responsive interfaces, APIs, data models, authentication, AI-enabled features, testing, deployment, and operational tooling.

My background in enterprise sales and customer-facing operations shapes how I approach engineering: I uncover the real problem, translate complex requirements, communicate tradeoffs clearly, and build products that work for the people using them. Recent projects include a private AI-assisted job-search CRM, a production acquisition platform, and a tenant-isolated learning system with bounded AI coaching and cited retrieval.
